Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026.

Document data

The document generators are designed to run in your browser. Information entered into document fields is used to update the on-page preview and is not intentionally submitted to a DocCraftTools document database.

Our hosting provider may process standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp and security events to operate and protect the website.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the website does not include advertising or analytics scripts by default. If that changes, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated before deployment.

External links

External websites operate under their own privacy policies.
