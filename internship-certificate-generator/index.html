What should an internship certificate include?

A useful internship certificate should identify the intern, confirm the placement dates and department, and briefly describe the work completed.

Use exact dates

Match the certificate dates to the company's internship or attendance records.

Describe real work

Summarize genuine responsibilities, projects and learning outcomes rather than using vague praise.

Keep the tone professional

Use clear, factual language that employers and educational institutions can easily verify.
