What should a relieving letter include?

A relieving letter should clearly identify the employee, confirm the employment period and final working day, and state that the employee has been released from their duties.

Confirm the final working day

Use the date recorded in the accepted resignation, separation or offboarding documents.

Record the employee's role

Include the job title, department and employee ID to make the letter easy to verify.

Complete the handover first

Only confirm clearance or release from duties when the employer's internal process has been completed.
