How to Calculate VAT: Add, Remove and Find the Tax Amount

VAT calculations depend on whether the starting amount excludes tax or already includes it. Using the correct formula prevents overstatement or understatement.

Add VAT to a net amount

Multiply the net amount by the VAT rate to find the tax. Then add the tax to the net amount. At 5%, a $1,000 net amount has $50 VAT and a $1,050 gross total.

Formula for adding VAT

VAT amount = net amount × rate. Gross amount = net amount + VAT amount. Convert the percentage to a decimal, so 5% becomes 0.05 and 20% becomes 0.20.

Remove VAT from a gross amount

Do not simply subtract the percentage from the gross amount. Divide the gross amount by 1 plus the rate. At 5%, $1,050 ÷ 1.05 = $1,000 net, leaving $50 VAT.

Find only the VAT portion

VAT portion = gross amount − net amount. You can also calculate gross × rate ÷ (1 + rate). For $1,050 at 5%, the result is $50.

Rounding

Round according to the rules applicable to your invoices and accounting system. Repeatedly rounding individual lines can produce a small difference from calculating tax on the subtotal.

Check the applicable rate

Rates, exemptions and registration obligations vary by jurisdiction and transaction. Confirm the current treatment with the relevant authority or a qualified tax professional.

Important: Document, accounting and tax requirements vary by location and circumstance. This guide provides general information and is not legal, tax or accounting advice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add 20% VAT?

Multiply the net amount by 0.20, then add that tax to the net amount.

Why is removing 20% not the same as subtracting 20%?

Because the 20% was calculated on the smaller net amount, not on the gross amount.

Can I use the calculator for sales tax?

The arithmetic may be similar when tax is added to a net price, but legal treatment and terminology differ.
