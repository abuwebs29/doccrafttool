How to Create an Invoice: Step-by-Step Guide

A practical guide to invoice numbers, line items, due dates, totals and payment terms.

What an invoice is

An invoice is a formal request for payment. It records the seller, the buyer, the products or services supplied, the amount due and the expected payment date.

Information to include

  • Your business name and contact details
  • The customer's name and contact details
  • A unique invoice number
  • The issue date and payment due date
  • Clear line-item descriptions, quantities and rates
  • Subtotal, tax, discounts and final total
  • Payment instructions and relevant terms

Step-by-step process

  1. Choose a consistent numbering system.
  2. Describe each service or product clearly.
  3. Check quantities, rates and tax calculations.
  4. Add a realistic payment deadline.
  5. Review spelling, contact details and totals before sending.

Common invoice mistakes

Avoid vague descriptions, duplicate invoice numbers, missing payment instructions and inconsistent totals. A concise invoice is easier for the customer to approve and pay.
