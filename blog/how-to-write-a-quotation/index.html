How to Write a Professional Quotation

Learn how to structure scope, pricing, validity and terms in a professional business quotation.

What a quotation does

A quotation gives a prospective customer a defined price for specified goods or services before the order is confirmed.

Key sections

  • Quotation number and issue date
  • Seller and customer details
  • Detailed scope or item descriptions
  • Quantities, rates, tax and total price
  • Validity period
  • Delivery, payment and acceptance terms

Write a clear scope

Describe exactly what is included. For services, mention deliverables, revisions and exclusions. For products, include model, quantity, unit price and delivery assumptions.

Set a validity date

A validity date protects both parties when prices, availability or scheduling may change. Common validity periods include 7, 14 or 30 days, depending on the work.

Avoid ambiguity

Do not leave important assumptions unwritten. Clear acceptance terms reduce disagreements and make it easier to convert the quotation into an invoice later.
