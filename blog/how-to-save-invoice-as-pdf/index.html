How to Save an Invoice as a PDF

PDF preserves the layout of an invoice across devices and is widely accepted for email and record keeping.

Complete the invoice first

Review the seller and customer details, invoice number, dates, line items, tax, total and payment instructions before creating the PDF.

Open the print dialog

Use the tool’s Print or Save as PDF button. In most browsers, you can also press Ctrl+P on Windows or Command+P on macOS.

Choose Save as PDF

Select “Save as PDF” or the equivalent PDF destination rather than a physical printer. Choose the correct page size and orientation.

Check print settings

Use default margins unless the preview clips content. Turn off unnecessary browser headers and footers if they add the page URL or date around the document.

Name the file clearly

A useful file name is “INV-1042-Acme-Aug-2026.pdf.” Avoid names such as “invoice-final-new2.pdf” that are difficult to search later.

Review the saved file

Open the PDF and check that no lines are cut off, totals are visible and the document is readable on a phone. Then send it with a concise payment email.

Important: Document, accounting and tax requirements vary by location and circumstance. This guide provides general information and is not legal, tax or accounting advice.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the page URL appearing on my PDF?

Disable browser headers and footers in the print settings.

Should I password-protect an invoice PDF?

Usually not necessary, but consider secure delivery when the document contains sensitive information.

Can the customer edit a PDF invoice?

Standard PDFs are less convenient to edit than source files, which is one reason they are commonly used for final invoices.
