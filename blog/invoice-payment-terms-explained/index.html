Invoice Payment Terms Explained: Net 7, Net 15, Net 30 and More

Payment terms tell a customer when and how an invoice should be paid. Clear terms reduce questions, prevent avoidable delays and give both sides a shared reference point.

What invoice payment terms mean

Payment terms are the conditions attached to a request for payment. They usually state the due date, accepted payment methods, currency, bank or online-payment instructions, and any consequences of late payment. Terms should be visible on the invoice rather than hidden in a separate message.

Common payment terms

Net 7 means payment is due seven calendar days after the invoice date. Net 15 and Net 30 work the same way. “Due on receipt” requests immediate payment, while “end of month” sets the deadline at the end of the invoice month. A specific date such as “Due August 15, 2026” is often clearest because it removes interpretation.

How to choose a due date

Choose a deadline that matches the size of the job, your cash-flow needs and the customer’s payment process. Small freelance projects may suit seven or fourteen days. Larger organizations may need thirty days because invoices pass through approval and accounts-payable teams. Agree on the timing before work begins whenever possible.

Example wording

A simple payment note could say: “Payment of $1,250 is due by August 15, 2026. Please include invoice number INV-1042 with your bank transfer.” Keep the language direct and professional. Avoid mixing several different deadlines in the same document.

Late fees and discounts

Only include late fees or early-payment discounts when they were agreed in advance and are permitted where you operate. State the percentage, calculation method and start date clearly. Because requirements vary by jurisdiction and contract, obtain professional advice when the amount or relationship is significant.

Final check

Before sending, confirm that the written payment term matches the calculated due date, the currency is correct and the payment details belong to the right account. A technically correct invoice can still be delayed by one incorrect digit in the payment instructions.

Important: Document, accounting and tax requirements vary by location and circumstance. This guide provides general information and is not legal, tax or accounting advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is Net 30 thirty business days?

Usually it means thirty calendar days unless the agreement says otherwise.

Should every invoice have payment terms?

Yes. Even a short statement such as a specific due date and accepted payment method is useful.

Can I change payment terms after sending an invoice?

You can ask, but material changes should normally be agreed with the customer rather than imposed unilaterally.
