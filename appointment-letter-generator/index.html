What should an appointment letter include?

A clear appointment letter should confirm the role, joining date, compensation, probation, working arrangements and acceptance terms.

State the employment terms

Include the job title, department, employment type, work location and start date.

Explain pay and benefits

Record the agreed salary and summarize benefits without contradicting the employment contract or company policy.

Check local requirements

Review applicable employment law and internal HR policies before issuing the final letter.
