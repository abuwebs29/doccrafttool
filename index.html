Frequently asked questions

Is DocCraftTools free?

Yes. The currently available generators can be used without creating an account or paying a subscription.

Can I save a document as PDF?

Yes. Use the print option in the generator and choose your browser's save-as-PDF destination.

Does DocCraftTools store my document information?

The core generators are designed to process document fields in the browser and do not require a document database.
