How to use this receipt generator

  1. Enter your business and recipient details.
  2. Add each product or service as a separate line item.
  3. Set tax, discount and document dates when needed.
  4. Review the live preview for accuracy.
  5. Select Print / Save PDF and choose your browser's PDF option.

What should a receipt generator include?

A clear receipt generator normally includes a unique number, issue date, business details, recipient details, item descriptions, quantities, prices, applicable tax, the final total and relevant payment or delivery terms.

Why use a browser-based tool?

A browser-based generator is useful when you need a document quickly without creating an account or installing software. This tool keeps the workflow simple and creates a printable result directly on the page.

Frequently asked questions

Is this receipt generator free?

Yes. The generator is free to use and does not require an account.

Can I save the document as a PDF?

Yes. Use the Print / Save PDF button, then choose your browser's save-as-PDF destination.

Is my document stored?

The generator works in your browser and does not require a server-side document account or database.
