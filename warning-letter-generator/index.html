What should an employee warning letter include?

A warning letter should describe the concern factually, state the expected improvement, set a reasonable review date and explain possible next steps.

Use specific facts

Record dates, actions and policies accurately without exaggeration or emotional language.

Explain the expected standard

Tell the employee what must change and how improvement will be assessed.

Follow company policy

Review internal disciplinary procedures and applicable employment law before issuing the letter.
