How VAT calculation works

To add VAT, multiply the net amount by the VAT rate and add the result to the net amount. To remove VAT from a gross amount, divide the gross amount by one plus the VAT rate.

Example

At a 5% VAT rate, a net amount of $100 produces $5 VAT and a gross amount of $105.

Important note

This calculator performs arithmetic only. VAT rules, registration thresholds and applicable rates vary by jurisdiction, so confirm the correct treatment with the relevant tax authority or a qualified adviser.
